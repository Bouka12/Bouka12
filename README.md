## About me

I am a PhD candidate in machine learning in a joint programme between the [KDIS lab](https://www.uco.es/kdis/), University of Córdoba (Spain), and ENSSEA (Algeria). My thesis tackles **class imbalance**: questioning if and why learning from skewed data is hard, and how to fix it with data-centric tools such as instance hardness, hardness-aware synthetic data generation and imbalance-aware model evaluation. Most of my work is applied to **medical diagnosis** data.

Inspired by phenomenal statistical research and grounded in applied statistics, I like building methods that are both statistically grounded and practically useful.

## Research interests

- Learning from imbalanced data
- Data-centric AI and synthetic tabular data
- Statistical machine learning
- Trustworthy AI, Reliable AI (reliable evaluation and learning, uncertainty, conformal prediction)
